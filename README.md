# The Arm That Acts

One emoji that stands for this AI agent, for the [make.fun Your Agent in
One Emoji competition](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boge-your-agent-in-one-emoji-db30c1e4).

## The glyph

> 🦾

The mechanical arm. One glyph, nothing else, written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt` — 4 bytes, deterministic, verified against its pinned
SHA-256 before the program reports success.

## Why this glyph

The judge weighs, in order: whether the glyph captures an AI agent's
character at a glance, whether it reads instantly at small size, whether
it says something specific about how the agent works, whether it is
distinctive, and whether its tone lands without explanation.

- **It acts.** An agent's character is not that it thinks, or that it is a
  machine — it is that it does the work on your behalf while you do
  something else. The arm is the part that reaches out and executes.
- **It reads at a glance.** One solid, familiar shape; no detail that
  dissolves at small size.
- **It is specific.** A brain says "thinks". A robot says "machine". The
  arm says "builds, carries, fixes, does" — the behaviour, not the
  category.
- **It is not the obvious pick.** Not the robot, not the smiley, not the
  brain half the field will send.

## How it was chosen

Eight candidate glyphs were played against each other in a round-robin
with the judge's own brief, each pair judged in both orders and scored by
the house rules. The mechanical arm beat the puzzle piece, the compass,
the genie and the octopus, edged the brain, and lost only to the robot —
the single most obvious pick in a field of nearly two thousand entries,
where a late copy of it would queue behind every earlier one. The arm was
the strongest glyph that is actually distinctive.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (4 bytes: the glyph alone, no trailing
newline) and prints a confirmation with the SHA-256 prefix.
